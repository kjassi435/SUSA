Refund Policy

Clear, simple refund terms for our application fee and store investment.

Last updated: 5 August 2026

1. Application registration fee — non-refundable

The one-time application registration fee of ₹4,999, paid at the time of submitting your franchise application, is strictly non-refundable.

The ₹4,999 application registration fee is non-refundable under any circumstances — whether your application is approved, rejected, or you withdraw from the process. This fee covers application processing, eligibility vetting, and preliminary consultation.

2. Store investment payments

Once an application is approved, further payments relate to your store project. These are covered by the following refund terms:

  • Full refund: If you cancel your project before any work begins (design, procurement, or fit-out), the store investment amount paid is fully refundable.
  • Non-refundable after work starts: Once design work, equipment procurement, or site fit-out has commenced, payments are non-refundable, as costs are committed to your project.
  • Partial refund: Where work has started but the project is cancelled by mutual agreement, an equitable refund may be offered based on work completed — determined on a case-by-case basis.

3. Equipment & materials

Equipment, machinery, and materials ordered for your store cannot be refunded once ordered, as they are procured and configured specifically for your project.

4. How to request a refund

To request a refund for an eligible store investment payment, email us at hello@susaenterprise.com with your application details. Requests should be made within 7 days of the payment or cancellation event.

  • Include your full name, application reference, and payment details
  • Refunds are processed within 7–10 business days of approval
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Gateway processing charges, if any, are deducted from the refund

5. Non-refundable items — summary

  • Application registration fee (₹4,999) — always non-refundable
  • Store investment, once design / procurement / fit-out has started
  • Equipment and materials once ordered
