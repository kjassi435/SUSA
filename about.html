Who We Are

From wheat to storefront

SUSA ENTERPRISE was born from a simple belief: a bakery should be as beautiful as the food it serves. We design, build, and deliver complete store concepts that make that belief a reality.

Our emblem — two wheat ears forming an oval wreath — represents the journey from seed to storefront. The gold of the wheat echoes warmth, craftsmanship, and the premium quality we bring to every project.

Our Journey

From idea to India's fastest growing bakery franchise

Baking delectable delicacies requires an uncompromising attitude, unbelievable craftsmanship, and unwavering passion for perfection. At SUSA, we believe and follow these principles in every aspect of our operation.

2012

The Beginning

SUSA ENTERPRISE was set up to redefine the concept of bakery franchises. Our vision was clear — to create turnkey bakery stores that combine exceptional design with premium quality.

2015

First Franchise Launch

Our first franchise store opened, establishing our turnkey model. The store exceeded all expectations, validating our design-led approach to bakery concepts.

2020

50+ Fit-Outs Delivered

Reached the milestone of 50+ complete store fit-outs. Expanded our product lines to 6 integrated categories and launched 4 store formats.

Today

Expanding Across India

Growing more than 150% year on year. Enabling everyone to celebrate every moment is our motto. We're expanding to more cities across India with our proven franchise model.
